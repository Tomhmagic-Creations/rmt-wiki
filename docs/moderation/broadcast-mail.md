# 🛡️ Broadcast mail

Send a saved message to registered players and review your broadcast history.


## 📌 Broadcast mail

Players with Mail All access can use the confirmed broadcast composer and separate broadcast history, or:

```text
/thm_rmt:mailall "The build competition starts tomorrow."
```

Broadcasts reach registered players, including offline players. Access, sending and broadcasting are separate permissions. Duplicate-message rules, cooldowns, recipient blocks and capacity can stop a delivery.

With mail privacy enabled in [Streamer Mode](/players/streamer-mode), opening masked mail does not mark it read.


## 📌 Send from the menu

Open Mail, choose **Mail All**, compose the message and review the confirmation. Broadcasts have their own searchable history, separate from private Outbox entries.

You need Mail access, sending and broadcasting powers. A full sent history refuses a new send until entries are removed; it does not silently replace old history. Deleting your history entry does not recall already delivered letters.

Use a scheduled Announcement for recurring online messages. Mail All is for saved letters to registered players, including offline accounts.
