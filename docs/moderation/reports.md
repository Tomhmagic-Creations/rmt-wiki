# 🛡️ Review reports

Investigate reports, record a decision and preserve a clear review history.


## 📌 Review one

1. Open **Moderation > Reports** and choose New or search for the report.
2. Read the details and set the status to Investigating while reviewing it.
3. Add staff notes and choose an outcome: Valid or Dismissed.
4. Mark the status Resolved when finished, then archive if desired.

**Status** tracks progress; **outcome** tracks the decision. Archiving preserves history and the decision. Permanent deletion is a separate confirmed action.

## 📋 Status and outcome are different

| Field | Choices | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | New, Investigating, Resolved | Tracks the progress of your review |
| Outcome | Unreviewed, Valid, Dismissed | Records the decision |

A resolved report can be valid or dismissed. Archiving preserves both fields; restoring returns it to the appropriate active status list. Staff notes and changes record the acting player and timestamp.

## ✅ A complete review

Read the report, inspect available evidence, record relevant notes, choose an outcome and resolve the status. Archive completed cases when appropriate. Permanent deletion removes the record and requires separate access and confirmation. Initial limits are 1,000 reports and 50 audit entries per report; full audit history rejects further changes rather than silently discarding old entries.
