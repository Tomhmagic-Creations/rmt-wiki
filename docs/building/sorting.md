# 🧱 Sorting inventories

Organise containers or your main inventory with the Chest Sorter and sorting commands.


## 📌 Sort a container

Use the **Chest Sorter** on a permitted block container or non-player entity container. It combines compatible stacks and orders items by type, with additional ordering for items such as potions and enchanted books.

The sorter item does not target another player's inventory. Claims, locks and normal interaction permissions still apply.

## 📌 Sort your own inventory

```text
/thm_rmt:sort self
```

Only your 27 main inventory slots are reordered. Your hotbar and equipment stay in place. Player sorting reorders stacks without combining them into your hotbar.

Look at a supported target within six blocks and use:

```text
/thm_rmt:sort block
/thm_rmt:sort entity
```
