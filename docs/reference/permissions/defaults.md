Exact nodes and descriptions for realm administrators configuring this area.

## Initial Group Grants

Role-owned first-setup grants, not recurring grants on every load. Every player resolves Default independently of staff membership. No entry grant is necessary for unrestricted dimensions. Ban-duration caps are absent by default, meaning unlimited. Legacy migration is separate.
