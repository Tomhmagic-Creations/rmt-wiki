# 🧭 Kits, crates and the store

Collect configured rewards and understand how payment and limits work.


## 📌 Claim a kit

Open **Kits** or run `/thm_rmt:kit`. Select an available kit and claim it. A kit can have a cooldown, redemption limit, group restriction and inventory-handling policy.

```text
/thm_rmt:kit list
/thm_rmt:kit claim starter
```

Make room first. Depending on the kit's policy, conflicts can relocate items, drop them or reject delivery. Your Ender Chest is not part of a kit.

## 📌 Open a crate

Interact once with an assigned crate block. A crate can require an authenticated key, ordinary items, a scoreboard balance, or no payment. A renamed lookalike is not an authentic key.

Cooldown and lifetime opening limits apply across all blocks assigned to the same crate. Payment and an opening count can be consumed before a reward reports a configuration failure. Ask staff to review a problem; repeated opening is not a recovery method.

## 📌 Buy from the Realm Store

Use its main-menu button when enabled, or `/thm_rmt:store` when permitted. Browse categories, inspect a product's payment and buy it. The owner may use inventory items, scoreboard points or an externally issued redemption code.

Check **My Purchases** for your receipts. Store rewards run as the buyer, so their commands must work with the buyer's permissions.

Ask staff if a reward or purchase reports a problem. Keep the receipt or crate/kit name so they can identify it.
