# 🧭 Realm warps

Visit shared destinations created for the realm.


## ❓ What is a realm warp?

A realm warp is a shared location managed by staff, such as spawn shops, an arena or a resource area. You browse the destinations your account can access; creating a personal destination uses Player Warps instead.

## 📌 Travel to a warp

1. Open **Warps** from the RMT menu.
2. Browse or search the available destinations and read the description.
3. Select the intended warp and wait for its travel warm-up.

```text
/thm_rmt:warps
/thm_rmt:warp market
```

Replace `market` with the exact saved warp name. Access is checked for the destination, not merely for knowing its name.

## ❓ Why is a warp missing or denied?

Some destinations are restricted to particular players or permission groups. Your realm may also restrict the Warps feature or command. Ask staff about the intended access rather than trying another person's account or a different selector.

## ⏳ During travel

Stay still if the realm cancels warm-ups for movement. Damage, dimension restrictions, borders or a forbidden claim entry can also stop travel. A staff edit that moves or removes the selected destination prevents using its old location.

Realm warps, Player Warps and shared team warps are separate systems. Look in the corresponding menu if you cannot find a friend's personal warp here.
