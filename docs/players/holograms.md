# 💬 Your holograms

Create and edit floating text when your realm allows personal displays.


## 📌 Place text

1. Use the **Place Hologram** item on a block.
2. Add text lines in the order you want them read.
3. Adjust position, scale, text/background colour and the offered display settings.
4. Save and check it in the world from a visitor's position.

Your account needs personal Hologram access and enough display capacity. The realm can set separate ownership and rendering limits; possessing the item alone does not remove them.

## ✏️ Edit your display

Use **Hologram Editor** on a block near your display. It selects the nearest manageable hologram within eight blocks, measured in three dimensions and in the same dimension. Your permitted personal displays may also be available through Profile.

Plain text does not need a click hitbox to be edited. When a display has an interaction hitbox, using the editor on it selects that exact hologram rather than activating the ordinary gameplay action.

## 🔎 Check visibility

Confirm the text, scale, position, visibility permission, render distance and expiry. A saved display is not necessarily visible from every distance or to every player. Nearby text displays also share rendering capacity.

Use placeholders only when you understand their subject. A world hologram is not a viewer-personal menu, and Streamer Mode does not automatically mask its text for everyone looking at it.
