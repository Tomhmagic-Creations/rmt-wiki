# 🧭 Profile and personal settings

Check your information and choose how RMT behaves for you.


Open **Profile** from the RMT menu. It shows your groups, join date, permitted playtime, claim allowance and unread mail. Dates shown here use UTC.

## 📌 Change a preference

1. Open **Profile > Personal Settings**.
2. Select the setting you want to change.
3. Make your choice and save. Back leaves an unsaved choice unchanged.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Incoming TPA | Who may ask to travel to you |
| Incoming TPHERE | Who may ask you to travel to them |
| Border Appearance | Your world-border texture and colour |
| Claim Borders | Private particles outlining nearby claims; initially off |
| Chunk Viewer | Private outlines of your current chunk; initially off |
| Streamer Mode | Optional masking for sensitive RMT displays |

For each request type, choose **Everyone**, **Teammates Only** or **Nobody**. TPA and TPHERE are independent. These choices do not grant travel permissions.

Your settings are personal. Changing a border colour does not change the realm border for other players. Removing your ability to edit a preference does not automatically reset its saved value.

See [Streamer Mode](/players/streamer-mode), [Mail](/players/mail) and [Stats](/players/stats) for the related features.

## 📋 Read your profile

Groups describe your assigned RMT roles, not necessarily native operator status. Claim Allowance separates what you have earned, received as bonus and used for land. The unread-mail count leads to your inbox; opening hidden mail under Streamer Mode does not mark it read.

## 🎨 Border appearance

Choose the permitted texture and colour in Border Appearance. Hex and RGB describe the same colour; saving your selection changes your display, not where the realm enforces its boundary. Reset affects only the appearance fields you are allowed to edit.

## ⭐ Quick access

Use Favourites to keep a shortcut to the personal tools you use most. A shortcut still checks current access; removing a feature permission does not make a saved shortcut a bypass.
