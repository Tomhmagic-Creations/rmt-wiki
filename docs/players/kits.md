# 🎒 Claim a kit

Receive a configured set of items when you meet its eligibility rules.


## 📌 Choose and claim

1. Open **Kits** in the RMT menu or enter `/thm_rmt:kit`.
2. Select a visible kit and read its restrictions.
3. Make room in your inventory and relevant equipment slots.
4. Claim once and inspect the received items.

```text
/thm_rmt:kit list
/thm_rmt:kit claim starter
```

Use the real kit ID in place of starter. Some kits are restricted to groups, have a redemption limit or require a cooldown. Give-only kits do not appear as normal claims.

## 📦 Where items go

Kits can contain main inventory, hotbar, armour and offhand items. They prefer the slots chosen when configured. Your Ender Chest is not part of the delivery.

The kit's conflict policy determines whether an occupied slot causes items to move, drop or fail delivery. Clearing space first makes the outcome easier to understand. Do not assume every kit simply adds loose items to any empty slot.

## ⏳ Cooldowns and redemptions

A cooldown counts from your last successful claim and continues while offline. A finite redemption limit is separate: waiting does not replenish a lifetime claim count. A disabled or group-restricted kit can remain unavailable even when the timer has ended.

## ❓ If a delivery is interrupted

Record the kit ID, the message and the items already present. Ask staff to inspect the delivery status before claiming again. A manual staff Give and a normal player claim use different eligibility rules, so one successful staff test does not establish that every player may claim it.
