# 🧭 Report a player

Send staff a clear account of a problem, including a player who is currently offline.


## 📌 Submit a report

```text
/thm_rmt:report "Player Name" "What happened and where"
```

The target must be a registered player and may be offline. Self-reports, repeated open reports and the submission cooldown can prevent a new report.


## 📌 Write a useful reason

Explain what happened, where it happened and roughly when. Name the actual player involved rather than a team or display label. A report asks staff to investigate; it does not prove that a rule was broken.

For example: `/thm_rmt:report "Player Name" "Broke the workshop chest near the east gate this afternoon"`.

## ❓ If the report is rejected

Check that the name belongs to a player known to the realm, that you are not reporting yourself, and that you do not already have an open report for the same target. Wait for the submission cooldown before trying another report. The initial cooldown is 60 seconds and reasons may contain up to 1,000 characters.

Staff review status and outcomes separately. Avoid submitting duplicates while waiting for a response.
