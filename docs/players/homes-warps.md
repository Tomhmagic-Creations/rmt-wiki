# 🧭 Homes and warps

Save personal destinations and travel to shared locations.


## 📌 Save a home

1. Stand where you want to return, facing the desired direction.
2. Open **Homes > Add**, enter a name and save.
3. Select that home later to start travelling to it.

The initial Default group allows five named homes. Your realm can change that limit. Home names have whitespace removed, so choose short, recognisable names. Setting a home must be allowed by the land's claim rules.

```text
/thm_rmt:sethome base
/thm_rmt:home base
/thm_rmt:homes
/thm_rmt:delhome base
```

The Homes menu can also show your current Overworld bed or Nether respawn anchor. These use your Minecraft spawn point and do not consume named home slots.

## 📌 Choose the right kind of warp

| Destination | Who manages it | Who can use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Realm warp | Realm administrators | Players with Warps access and permission for that warp |
| Player warp | Its player owner | Other permitted players, subject to its Allow Team choice |
| Shared team warp | Team owner/admins | Current teammates with team-warp access |

Open **Warps** for realm destinations or **Player Warps** for player-created ones. To create a player warp, use its add workflow at the intended location. Choose **Allow Team** when it should be limited to your current teammates. You can always use your own player warp; having no team does not create a public audience.

Deleting or changing a destination while somebody is warming up prevents that pending trip from completing to the old location.

Realm warps are managed by staff. Ask them if a shared destination is missing or no longer usable.
