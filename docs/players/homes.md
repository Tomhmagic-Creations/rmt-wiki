# 🏠 Homes

Save a private destination so you can return to it later.


## ❓ What is a home?

A home is a named location saved for you, including its dimension and the direction you were facing. It is different from a public player warp, a shared team home and the realm's Spawn destination.

## 📌 Save your first home

1. Stand at the exact arrival position, facing the intended direction.
2. Open **Homes > Add** and enter a short name such as `base`.
3. Save, then select the home from your home list to test it.

Names have whitespace removed. A blank name is rejected, and an existing name cannot be duplicated. The initial ordinary limit is five named homes, although your realm may change it. Saving must be allowed by the claim's Set Home rule.

## ⌨️ Use chat commands

```text
/thm_rmt:sethome base
/thm_rmt:homes
/thm_rmt:home base
/thm_rmt:delhome base
```

Use the exact saved name. Deleting removes the destination, not the building. The commands need their corresponding access as well as Home feature access.

## 🛏️ Bed and respawn-anchor destinations

The menu can also offer your current Overworld bed or Nether respawn anchor. These use your current Minecraft spawn point and do not occupy an extra named home slot. If no valid point exists, the relevant destination is not offered.

## ❓ If a home does not work

Check your home count, the claim's Set Home rule and the destination dimension. A warm-up can be cancelled by movement or damage under the realm's policy. Changing or deleting that home during travel prevents arrival at the old location. Use Cancel TP if you need to stop the trip yourself.
