# 📊 Your statistics

Read the gameplay totals the realm makes visible in Profile.


1. Open **Profile > Stats**.
2. Search or browse the visible stat names.
3. Open the relevant entry when more information is offered.

Your realm may track kills, deaths, playtime, particular block actions or custom goals. The owner chooses the definitions and which are visible. A hidden stat is not necessarily disabled, and you cannot create or reset definitions from your personal stat list.

## ⏱️ Playtime

Playtime is counted in active seconds and excludes AFK under RMT's policy. Time connected and time counted as active can therefore differ. A milestone using 3,600 seconds means one hour of eligible playtime, not necessarily one hour since joining.

## ❓ A value is missing

External addon stats may show unavailable rather than zero while their source is absent. A scoreboard-backed stat also needs a valid observed value. Do not assume that a question mark or missing row means an action total was reset to zero.

## 📌 Totals are not retroactive reconstruction

A newly created or changed stat tracks its configured actions going forward. It does not discover every matching action from before it existed. Ask staff about the realm's definition if a total differs from what you expected.
