# 🧭 Teams

Create a group of players who can share travel, markers and claim access.


Open **Teams** from the main menu, or run `/thm_rmt:team`. You can belong to one team at a time.

## 📌 Start or join a team

1. Open Teams and choose the creation option, browse public teams, or open your invitations.
2. Choose a unique name when creating a team.
3. Invite registered players from your team page. They can be offline; invitations last seven days.
4. Set a team home and create shared warps or waypoints where your access permits.

## 📌 Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Assign admins, transfer ownership and delete the team |
| Admin | Edit settings, manage invitations and ordinary members, and manage shared destinations |
| Member | Use the team's available features |

Admins cannot remove the owner or one another. The owner must transfer ownership or delete the team before leaving. A team can have up to 64 members.

## 📌 Use your team elsewhere

- Choose the **Team** audience for suitable [claim rules](/claims/rules-trust).
- Set a player warp's **Allow Team** option for teammate access.
- Limit incoming TPA or TPHERE to teammates in Personal Settings.
- Use shared team homes, warps and waypoints.
- Send a message with `/thm_rmt:teammsg "Meet at the base"` when permitted.

Friendly-fire controls appear only in the realm's Team or Team and Toggle PvP modes. Allowing friendly fire never overrides a claim's PvP protection. Team-based access follows current membership, including after someone leaves.

## 🏠 Set up shared travel

The team owner or an authorised team admin can set the home at the intended location and manage shared team warps. Members use them only while they remain on the team and have the relevant feature access. A team waypoint is a navigation marker, not an automatic teleport.

## 🔄 Leave or transfer ownership

An ordinary member can leave through the team controls. Owners must first transfer ownership or delete the team. Review any claim or player warp using Team access before changing membership, because those audiences follow current membership rather than a permanent invite list.

## ❓ Why an invitation or action fails

You can belong to only one team. An invitation expires after seven days, and a team has a maximum of 64 members. Team admins cannot remove the owner or one another. A missing friendly-fire option can be expected when the realm uses a PvP mode without team controls.
