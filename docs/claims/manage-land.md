# 🛡️ Remove, merge or transfer land

Change ownership and coverage without confusing land protection with world blocks.


## 🗑️ Remove chunks

1. Equip the **Claim Removal Tool**.
2. Use it on a chunk to remove one chunk, or sneak-use two corner chunks to select a rectangle.
3. Review any confirmation before removing the whole remaining claim.

This removes **claim protection**, not the physical blocks. Unclaimed chunks are skipped. The selection must belong to one claim you may manage. Ordinary claims enforce edge and connecting-land restrictions; a rectangle that cannot be removed legally is rejected.

Removing every remaining chunk needs the whole-claim administrative management access and confirmation. It also deletes that claim's settings and subclaims. If you only want to change who can enter, edit a rule instead.

## 📌 Merge and transfer

Use the claim management options available to your role. A merge or transfer checks ownership, access and the relevant land/accounting conditions. Do not assume that a trusted player or manager can perform every owner/admin action.

Transfers change the owner used by Team rules. Check trust, rules, allowance and waypoints after an ownership change.

## 🔄 What a transfer changes

A transfer keeps the claim’s ID, rules and subclaims. The recipient must have room in their available allowance for the land. The main owner changes, so Team audiences follow the new owner’s current team. Review shared access before confirming.

## 🗑️ Remove a subclaim or remove land?

Removing a subclaim removes its separate settings and membership area, while the parent still owns the chunks. Removing chunks from the main claim releases protection on that land and reduces Used allowance. Deleting the whole claim also deletes its settings and subclaims. None of these operations removes the physical buildings.
