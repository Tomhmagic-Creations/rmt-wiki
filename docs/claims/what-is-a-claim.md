# 🛡️ Claims and subclaims

A claim is land you protect so other players can only use it in the ways you allow.


## ❓ What is a claim?

A claim groups protected Minecraft chunks under one owner. You choose who may build, break, use containers and perform other available actions there. Buildings and chests inside that land follow its claim rules, alongside any separate locks or realm restrictions.

A **chunk** is a 16 × 16 block area. Claiming a chunk protects land rather than one individual building: a house, garden and workshop in the same claimed area can share the same rules. The realm sets the protected height range for each dimension.

In V2, your primary claim can contain land in different places and dimensions. The ordinary claim command reuses your existing primary claim when adding distant land. Do not use the old V1 rule of “one main claim per dimension” to interpret the V2 menus.

## 🗺️ How claimed chunks fit together

![Illustration of several chunks belonging to a claim](/assets/images/claims/claim.png)

This illustration shows how several chunks can belong to one protected area. The important distinction is the land belonging to the claim, not whether it contains a finished building. Check your current land and dimension in the claim menu before editing rules.

## 🔄 What is a subclaim?

A subclaim is a smaller named area inside a main claim, with its own trusted list, rules and environment options. It is useful when one workshop or house needs different access from the rest of your base.

![Illustration of selected chunks forming a subclaim](/assets/images/claims/subclaim.png)

For example, trust a friend in a workshop subclaim without opening every chest in your main base. A chunk cannot belong to overlapping subclaims. Removing the subclaim does not unclaim the parent land.

## 🔐 Who can use the land?

For each available action rule, choose **None**, **Trusted**, **All** or **Team**. Trust gives someone the access allowed by those rules; it does not make them the owner or a claim manager. A Team audience uses the main owner's current team, including inside a subclaim.

Rules can differ between actions. You can allow visitors to enter while keeping building and containers private. Some realm-enforced rules or options are read-only for players.

## 📦 What pays for land?

Each newly claimed chunk uses one available **Claim Allowance**. A subclaim over land you already own does not charge for that land again. Read the balance in Profile before making a large selection.

## ▶️ Where to start

Use **Create a claim** for the flag controls, then **Rules and trust** to share access. The Claims dropdown lists these guides separately so you can return directly to the task you need.
