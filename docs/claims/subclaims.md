# 🛡️ Subclaims

Give a smaller part of your land its own trust list and rules.


1. Use a **Sub Claim Flag** on a chunk within a claim you can manage, or sneak-use two corner chunks for an area.
2. Choose or create the intended subclaim in the offered workflow.
3. Name it, then configure its trusted players, rules and options.

The subclaim belongs inside one main claim. Its chunks cannot overlap another subclaim, and a selection cannot include a different main claim, even if you own both.

## 📌 When the selection includes unclaimed chunks

| Choice | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Skip Unclaimed Chunks | Uses only eligible land already in the parent claim; costs no additional allowance |
| Claim Unclaimed Chunks | Expands that parent and includes the new land; charges the main owner's allowance |

At least one selected chunk must already be in the parent claim. The form shows the additional cost before you choose. The parent owner pays for expansion, not necessarily the person holding the flag.

## 💡 Example use

Use a subclaim for a friend's workshop inside your base. Give its trusted list workshop access while keeping the rest of your base private. For a complete shared settlement, see [Build a team base](/tutorials/team-base).

Subclaims do not create a separate team reference: Team rules still use the main claim owner's team.
