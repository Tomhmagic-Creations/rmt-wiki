# ⚙️ Claiming

Set the starting land allowance, earning interval and default protection policy.

## 📌 Save the category

Open **Admin > Settings > Claiming**. Set the values below, then **Save Settings**. The separate **Claim Protection Heights** button opens per-dimension height settings.

## Starting Claim Allowance

The starting entitlement for new players. This is not an extra award every time they connect and does not overwrite existing balances.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 10 · **Range:** 0 to 100; increments of 1

## Claim Allowance Earn Frequency

Minutes of eligible active time per automatic earning interval. Earning amount and lifetime cap are permission values, not controls in this category. Changing the frequency restarts partial interval progress.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 60 · **Range:** 30 to 300; increments of 10

## Claim Entry/Exit Messages

Show an action-bar message when players enter or leave a claim, even when their personal claim-border display is off.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Claim Safe Radius

Minimum spacing policy for making new claims near another player’s claim, measured in chunks. This does not change the size of an already claimed chunk.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 10 · **Range:** 1 to 100; increments of 1

## Default: Monster Spawning

Choose whether hostile creatures can spawn in the claim by default.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Default: Explosions

Choose whether explosions can affect the claim by default.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** Off

## Default: Entity Damage to Players

Choose whether entities can damage players in the claim by default.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Default: Player Damage to Players

Choose whether players can damage other players in the claim by default.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** Off

## Allow Editing: Monster Spawning

Allow claim owners to change the monster spawning option.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Allow Editing: Explosions

Allow claim owners to change the explosions option.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Allow Editing: Entity Damage to Players

Allow claim owners to change the entity damage to players option.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Allow Editing: Player Damage to Players

Allow claim owners to change the player damage to players option.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## 🔐 Default options and editing rights

For each environment option, its **Default** toggle chooses the realm value. The corresponding **Allow Editing** toggle decides whether claim owners may change it. When owner editing is disabled, the option follows realm policy. Player claim rules and these environment options have different jobs.

## 📏 Claim Protection Heights

The button in this category opens the per-dimension height editor. Use the dedicated Claim Protection Heights guide in Settings navigation for the complete procedure.

## 📌 Allowance limits are elsewhere

Set **earn amount** and **lifetime automatic-earnings cap** in permissions. Staff grants and balance edits are explained in Admin > Configure Claim Allowance. The frequency control alone does not grant earning access.
