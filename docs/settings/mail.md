# ⚙️ Mail

Choose duplicate-message protection and mailbox capacities.

## 📌 Save the category

Open **Admin > Settings > Mail** and finish with **Save Settings**. These capacities and duplicate rules apply to all players; personal sender blocks are managed by each player in Mail.

## Mail Duplicate Window (Minutes)

How long identical letters to the same recipient are blocked, even after deletion.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 60 · **Range:** 1 to 60; increments of 1

## Mail Inbox Limit

Maximum stored letters per player. A full inbox refuses new mail; existing mail is never automatically removed.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 100 · **Range:** 10 to 200; increments of 10

## Mail Sent-History Limit

Maximum entries in each player's private outbox and, separately, broadcast history. Old entries must be removed manually.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 100 · **Range:** 10 to 200; increments of 10

## 💡 Full histories do not silently discard old letters

A full inbox refuses incoming mail. A full private Outbox or broadcast history prevents a new send until its owner removes entries. Each sent-history limit applies independently. Deleting a message does not evade the duplicate-message window or recall a delivered letter.

## 📌 Sending access is separate

Permissions control who may read, send or broadcast. Mail cooldown is a permission value, distinct from the duplicate window. Do not increase these capacities to troubleshoot a missing send permission.
