# ⚙️ Teleport

Choose what can cancel travel and where random teleport may search.

## 📌 Save the category

Open **Admin > Settings > Teleport**, choose the cancellation policy and **Save Settings**. These controls apply to RMT travel, rather than every native Minecraft or other-addon teleport.

## Cancel TP on Damage

Master control for cancelling travel because of damage. The individual damage toggles refine which damage types apply when this policy is enabled.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP Damage Cooldown

Seconds after taking relevant damage before the player can teleport. This is separate from the warm-up before departure.

**Control:** Slider · **Default:** 5 · **Range:** 1 to 60; increments of 1

## Cancel TP on Movement

Cancel the supported travel warm-up when the player moves.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## RTP Avoid Claims

Keep RTP searches out of every claim, including claims the traveller could enter. Turning this off does not bypass a claim’s entry denial.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Block Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes block damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Entity Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes entity damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Player Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes player damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Projectile Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes projectile damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Environment Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes environment damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## Cancel TP on Other Damage

Cancel teleport if the player takes other damage.

**Control:** Toggle · **Default:** On

## 📋 Damage types

The remaining toggles cover block, entity, player, projectile, environment and other damage. Review the master damage setting first, then enable only the relevant types for your realm. The recent-damage cooldown is separate from a feature’s reuse cooldown.

## 📌 Warm-ups and RTP limits are permissions

Teleport delay, RTP radius, cooldown and allowed dimensions are assigned per group/player. They are not missing fields in this category. Safe landing, borders and claim-entry checks still apply when movement cancellation is disabled.
